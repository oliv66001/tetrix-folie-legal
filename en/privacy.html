Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Data controller and contact

The data controller is Développement-Solution — Olivier Pagé, 20 rue Condorcet, 66330 Cabestany, France.

For any question about data: developpement.solution@gmail.com.

Data stored by the game

The game stores on the device only: the best score, campaign progress (completed missions and stars earned) and the settings (language, board rotation mode, ghost piece display, music and sound effects volume). This data is never sent to the publisher.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, age or location, and does not access contacts, microphone, camera or files. It has no chat.

Google Play Games (achievements and leaderboard)

At launch, the game tries to sign you in automatically to Google Play Games, Google’s service for achievements and leaderboards. If you are signed in, the game sends Google the achievements you unlock and your endless-mode best score; Google links them to your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and shows the leaderboard to other players according to your Play Games visibility settings. The publisher does not receive your email address and stores none of this data on its own servers.

This processing is carried out by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited under its own privacy policy. You can decline sign-in, manage your profile visibility or delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings; the game works fully without signing in.

Advertising, purchases and analytics

Ads. The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): rewarded videos that you choose to watch (continue a game, double points, start with a shield) and, at most once every few games, a full-screen ad between two games. To serve and measure these ads, AdMob processes device data: advertising ID, IP address, technical device information and ad interactions, under Google’s advertising policy.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a certified consent form (Google UMP) is shown before any ad. You can refuse personalised ads; non-personalised ads may then be shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings (Google → Ads).

In-app purchase. The “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. The publisher receives neither your payment details nor your email address; the game only stores on the device that the purchase was made.

The game includes no other analytics or tracking tool.

External links

Links from the game to these pages open in your browser. GitHub Pages, which hosts these pages, may record technical information (IP address, browser type) in its security logs, under its own privacy policy.

Children

Starfuse is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us data (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Email support

When you contact the publisher, your email address, the content of your message and any attachments are processed to answer your request, handle an incident or exercise your rights. The legal basis is the publisher’s legitimate interest in providing support.

This data is accessible to the publisher and to the email provider used. It is kept as long as needed to handle the request, then at most three years after the last exchange, unless a legal obligation or dispute requires otherwise. Do not send passwords or bank details by email.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Game data stays on the device until you erase it (in the game or in the phone settings) or uninstall the game: see the account and data deletion page. For support exchanges, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability where applicable law provides for it, at the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or with your local authority.

Security

The game limits its data to what is described above. Support messages are accessible only to the publisher. No system is completely risk-free; do not send unnecessary sensitive data.

Changes

This policy may change, in particular if ads, purchases, a player account or an online mode are added. The date at the top of the page will be updated and, if the change is significant, it will be announced in the game or on the Google Play listing.
