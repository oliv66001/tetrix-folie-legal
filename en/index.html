Tetrix Folie

Legal information and support

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Tetrix Folie is a falling-block game played on a cross-shaped board. Each arm of the cross is a world with its own gravity, and the board turns regularly to send you to the next world. The blocks are glasses filled with liquid whose colours mix together.

Publisher: Développement-Solution — Olivier Pagé, France.

The current version stores the best score and settings on the device only. It does not create a player account, shows no ads and offers no purchases. These pages will be updated before any change affecting data.
