Account and data deletion

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data of Starfuse, published by Développement-Solution — Olivier Pagé.

Player account

Starfuse does not create any account with the publisher: there is no username, password or profile stored on a publisher server. There is therefore no account to delete on the publisher’s side.

If you use Google Play Games, your achievements and leaderboard score are linked to your Play Games profile, managed by Google. To delete them: Google Play Games app → Settings → delete your Play Games account and data, or use your Google Account settings.

Delete game data

The best score, campaign progress and settings are stored on the device only. To erase them:

  • In the game: home screen → Settings → Erase my data, then confirm.
  • On Android: phone Settings → Apps → Starfuse → Storage → Clear data.

Uninstalling the game also deletes this data. Labels may vary by phone; also consider any automatic system backups (Google backup).

Request deletion of data held by the publisher

If you have written to support, you can request the deletion of your messages and email address:

Request deletion of my data

  • Data deleted: your messages, attachments and email address.
  • Timeframe: within 30 days of receiving your request. A confirmation is sent to you.
  • Possible retention: only what a legal obligation or an ongoing dispute requires us to keep, and only for as long as necessary.

If an account is added later

If a future version adds a player account, the game will offer account deletion directly in the app, and this page will describe the procedure, the data deleted, the timeframes and any retention.
